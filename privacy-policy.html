Privacy Policy

Unisko Assistant · Last updated: 11 August 2026

Unisko ("we", "us", "our") operates the Unisko Assistant, an automated WhatsApp service that answers questions from parents and sends reminders about payments. This policy explains what information the service handles and how we use it.

Information we handle

When you message the Unisko Assistant on WhatsApp, we receive:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The content of the messages you send to us

If your child is enrolled with Unisko, we also hold billing information provided to us directly, such as your name, your child's name, and any outstanding balance. This information is entered by Unisko staff and is not collected through the WhatsApp service.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions automatically
  • To pass questions we cannot answer to a member of Unisko staff, who will then reply to you
  • To send you reminders about outstanding payments

Third-party services

To provide the service, your messages are processed by:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp), which delivers messages between you and us
  • Google (Gemini AI), which generates answers to your questions
  • Google Sheets, where we store enrolment and billing records and a log of questions that need a staff reply

Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy policy.

Data retention

Questions that need a staff response are logged together with your phone number and the date. Billing records are kept for as long as your child is enrolled with Unisko. The service does not keep a history of conversations it has already answered.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
